# Air Pollution and Equity Returns in an Emerging Market

Replication code for *Air Pollution and Equity Returns in an Emerging Market:
Evidence from Kazakhstan.*

The paper asks whether daily variation in fine particulate matter in Almaty
moves returns on the Kazakhstan Stock Exchange (KASE) Index, instrumenting
pollution with meteorological dispersion conditions generated by the Tian Shan
basin. It finds no detectable effect, and reports formal equivalence bounds
rather than stopping at insignificance.

**Paper:** [SSRN link]
**Author:** Arnay Mukhtar

---

## Headline result

| | |
|---|---|
| Sample | 977 trading days, 10 Apr 2020 – 13 Nov 2025 |
| IV estimate on log PM2.5 | **−0.0766** (SE 0.1153), p = 0.506 |
| 95% CI | [−0.303, 0.149] |
| First-stage F | 135.6 |
| Sargan p | 0.117 |
| Equivalence bound | **0.185 pp** per doubling of PM2.5 |

The point estimate is negative, consistent in sign with the hypothesis that
pollution depresses returns, and statistically indistinguishable from zero.
Applying two one-sided tests, effects larger than 0.185 percentage points per
doubling are rejected at the five per cent level.

Mean PM2.5 over the sample is 29.8 µg/m³ — roughly six times the WHO annual
guideline, and 2.6 times the mean in the most-cited comparison study.

## Design

**Identification.** Pollution is endogenous to returns: it is generated by
industrial output, traffic and residential heating, all of which respond to
local economic conditions. The OLS estimate here is **positive** (+0.053),
exactly the sign that economic-activity confounding predicts. Instrumenting
reverses it.

The instruments are planetary boundary layer height, orthogonal wind
components, and surface pressure. Almaty sits in a basin bounded to the south
by the Trans-Ili Alatau, which generates frequent winter temperature
inversions. Boundary layer height in the sample ranges from 19 to 1,303
metres — on the most stable days the city's entire emissions load is trapped
beneath a layer thinner than a twenty-storey building. Whether a given January
day produces a deep inversion depends on synoptic conditions, not on anything
happening in the economy.

Four instruments for one endogenous regressor means the design is
overidentified and the exclusion restriction is partially testable.

**Equivalence, not significance.** Because the result is a null, the estimand
of interest is the exclusion bound, not the point estimate or its p-value.
Every robustness row reports `(|b| + 1.645·SE) · ln2` in percentage points per
doubling.

**Transport tests.** The benchmark specification is linear in concentration,
so applying it at 2.6 times its own mean admits two hypotheses. Both estimates
carry uncertainty, so equality of the two parameters is tested directly rather
than asking whether one falls inside the other's interval — dividing by our own
standard error alone would overstate the rejection.

```
src/
  01_fetch_pollution.py    OpenAQ, US Embassy monitor (daily + hourly)
  02_fetch_weather.py      ERA5 via Open-Meteo, hourly
  03_build_panel.py        exposure window, merge, fixed effects
  04_analysis.py           all tables, equivalence, placebos, RI
  05_figures.py            figures 1-5
data/                      inputs and built panels (see data/README.md)
output/                    results.txt and figures
```

Run in order:

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
export OPENAQ_API_KEY=...          # free, from explore.openaq.org

python src/01_fetch_pollution.py
python src/02_fetch_weather.py
python src/03_build_panel.py
python src/04_analysis.py
python src/05_figures.py
```

Steps 1 and 2 hit external APIs and take a few minutes. Steps 3–5 run offline
on the cached CSVs, so once the data is built the analysis reproduces in
seconds.

## Measurement choices worth flagging

**Exposure window.** Pollution is averaged over 07:00–17:59 local time,
requiring at least eight hourly observations. KASE equity trading runs from an
opening auction at 11:20 to a 17:30 close, so the window includes pre-session
exposure on the grounds that mood formation precedes trading. A calendar-day
mean would include post-close hours that cannot affect that day's price,
introducing measurement error in the treatment that attenuates toward zero —
a mechanical explanation for a null that has nothing to do with market
structure.

Local time is read off the timestamp string rather than converted from UTC,
which preserves Kazakhstan's move from UTC+6 to UTC+5 on 1 March 2024.

**Returns.** Computed from closing prices as `100·(ln P_t − ln P_{t−1})`
rather than taken from the source's rounded percentage-change column. Rounding
would introduce classical measurement error in the dependent variable — a
particular concern in a paper whose central claim is an absence.

**Wind direction.** A circular variable, so it is decomposed into orthogonal
components before averaging. The arithmetic mean of 350° and 10° is 180°, the
opposite of the true resultant direction.

**Single monitor.** OpenAQ lists 192 PM2.5 sensors within 25 km of central
Almaty, but almost all came online in 2024–25 and belong to a low-cost network
with different calibration properties. Only the Embassy monitor spans the full
period. This trades spatial coverage for measurement quality; spatial
heterogeneity in exposure is unobserved.

**Inference.** HAC (kernel) standard errors, validated by randomisation
inference — the return series is circularly shifted 1,000 times in multiples of
five days to preserve weekday alignment. The resulting null distribution has a
standard deviation of 0.113 against a reported HAC standard error of 0.120, so
inference is accurate and marginally conservative.

## Known limitations

These are in the paper and are repeated here so the code isn't read as
claiming more than it does.

- **The placebo pattern is unresolved.** The one-day lead estimate exceeds the
  contemporaneous estimate in magnitude, and four of five lead coefficients are
  negative where noise should give roughly a coin flip. This is consistent with
  residual low-frequency co-movement the controls do not absorb, or with the
  design simply lacking resolution. The two cannot be separated with this data.
- **Instrument sensitivity is material.** Sargan does not reject (p = 0.117),
  but the tension concentrates in the meridional wind component; dropping it
  raises the statistic to p = 0.760. The four-instrument set is retained as
  preferred rather than selecting instruments on results, but it was not
  pre-registered.
- **Power.** Attaining a bound of 0.05 return SD per doubling under this design
  would need roughly 16,300 trading days — over sixty-five years.
- **Sample period.** Opens in April 2020 and includes both the pandemic and the
  January 2022 unrest. Both are controlled for and results are insensitive to
  excluding either, but the period is not a normal one.
